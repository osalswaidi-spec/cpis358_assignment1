Who We Are

About OctoDrives

OctoDrives was created to modernize the car test-drive journey. By combining OCR, workflow automation, and a clean interface, the platform removes paperwork and makes the experience faster and more reliable.

Our Mission

To deliver a contactless, secure, and time-efficient test-drive experience that benefits both customers and dealerships.

Our Vision

To become the leading digital test-drive platform in the Kingdom, adopted by dealerships across the country.
